Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Petal Planner (“we”, “us”) is a daily task planner built and operated by Musfira Mubeen in Pakistan. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text) and an optional display name.
  • Planner content: the tasks, notes, challenges, rewards, petals and settings you create in the app.
  • WhatsApp details (only if you link WhatsApp): your WhatsApp phone number, the text of messages you send to our WhatsApp number, and the time of your last message. We use these to create your tasks and send your reminders.

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not collect your contacts, location or files.

How we use your information

  • To run the planner and keep your data in sync between your devices.
  • To turn your WhatsApp messages into tasks and to send the reminders you ask for.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): messages sent to and from our WhatsApp number pass through WhatsApp and Meta's Cloud API, and are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Render: our server and database are hosted on Render's cloud platform.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account, planner content and linked WhatsApp number from our database.

Your choices

  • You can stop messaging us at any time, and you can ask us by email to unlink your WhatsApp number.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing musfiramubeen553@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We protect data with encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords. No online service is completely risk-free, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Petal Planner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
