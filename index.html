A gentler way to get things done.

Petal Planner is a daily task planner that grows a little garden as you finish your tasks. Add tasks in the app, or just send a WhatsApp message and it shows up in your planner with a reminder.

Daily planner

Plan tasks by day, priority and time, and review each day in the tracker.

Petals & rewards

Earn petals for finished tasks and spend them on rewards you choose yourself.

Challenges

Build habits with multi-day challenges and watch your streak grow.

WhatsApp quick-add

Text a task like “pay bill tomorrow 5pm” and get a reminder at that time.

How the WhatsApp feature works

After you sign up, you can link your own WhatsApp number inside the app. When you send a message to the Petal Planner WhatsApp number, we turn it into a task in your planner. If you included a time, we send you a reminder on WhatsApp at that time. You can ask us to unlink your number or delete your data at any time by email.
